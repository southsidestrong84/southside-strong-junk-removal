What Midlothian's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Midlothian's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm on the village's own pages.

Who collects the garbage

Republic Services, under the village's refuse contract. Pickup day is Tuesday.

Exact wording: “Republic provides trash and recycling pickup to the Village of Midlothian. Our contracted refuse pick-up day is TUESDAYS.”

Source: villageofmidlothian.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Republic picks up one large item per household per week, such as a couch, chair or recliner. Any metal parts or framing must be removed before the item goes to the curb. The page does not mention calling ahead.

Exact wording: “Republic Services will pick up 1 (one) large item per household per week. This includes furniture such as couches, chairs, recliners, etc. Note: If items have any metal parts or framing, they will need to be removed before placing them at the curb.”

Source: villageofmidlothian.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

April 1 to November 30. Yard waste goes loose in your container with a yard waste sticker on the container where the driver can see it. Residents can ask Republic (708-385-8252) for a used yard waste cart while supplies last, or use paper lawn bags. Trimmed branches must be bundled in sections no longer than four feet.

Source: villageofmidlothian.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Curbside e-waste pickup by Republic Services of Crestwood for Midlothian residents only, under a village agreement that started March 1, 2025. Call 708-385-8252 (or email Republic) 48 hours before your regular garbage day. Pickup is no more than once a month, and only household items qualify.

Source: villageofmidlothian.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Midlothian

Republic Services, 708-385-8252 (the village's number for refuse questions)

Source: villageofmidlothian.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

We could not confirm Midlothian's current rules on appliances, remodeling debris, hazardous waste. Ask Republic Services, 708-385-8252 (the village's number for refuse questions), before you set those out.
