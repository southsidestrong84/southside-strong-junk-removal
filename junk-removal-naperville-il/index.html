What Naperville's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Naperville's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the city's own site or its hauler's page for Naperville, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Groot provides residential garbage and recycling pickup in Naperville. Trash and recycling carts are purchased through the City.

Exact wording: “Residents of Naperville can choose from the below options for our refuse service. All trash & recycle carts must be purchased through the city.”

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Up to two bulk items a week, such as furniture, boxes, barrels and crates that one driver can lift, can go out with regular garbage collection. Appliances and oversized or overweight items do not count as bulk items.

Exact wording: “Large Items - Up to two bulk items (excluding appliances and oversized or overweight items) may be discarded per week. Bulk items include boxes, barrels, crates, furniture, and similar items that do not fit into a proper container and can be lifted by one driver without assistance.”

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

One appliance per week is picked up as part of regular service, but the pickup must be scheduled with Groot in advance.

Exact wording: “Up to two items per week are included with service. One appliance per week is included, but it must be scheduled in advance.”

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Weekly yard waste collection runs from mid-March to mid-December. Each biodegradable yard waste bag needs a sticker, and brush must be bundled and stickered. In November, yard waste is collected without stickers at no charge.

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Groot collects electronics at the curb when the pickup is prepaid and scheduled 48 hours before your regular service day.

Exact wording: “Groot provides curbside collection of e-waste items that have been prepaid and scheduled 48 hours in advance of your regularly scheduled service day.”

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Remodeling and construction debris

Limited remodeling and construction materials (rolled carpet, bathroom fixtures, cabinets, wood or drywall) are collected weekly at no extra charge, up to two units a week (two 32-gallon containers, bundles or carpet rolls, in any mix). Pieces must be no longer than 4 feet, no more than 2 feet in diameter and no heavier than 60 pounds each.

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

Naperville's Household Hazardous Waste drop-off at 156 Fort Hill Drive is open 9am to 2pm every Saturday and Sunday. Any Illinois resident may use it for household (not business) hazardous waste.

Exact wording: “All Illinois residents may use this site regardless of county or municipal residency, but it is for waste generated through household/residential operations (i.e., no business waste is accepted). Location: 156 Fort Hill Drive, Naperville, IL 60540 Hours: 9am - 2pm every Saturday and Sunday”

Source: www.kanecountyil.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Naperville

Naperville Public Works, 630-420-6095

Source: www.kanecountyil.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026
