What Oak Forest's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Oak Forest's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the city's own site or its hauler's page for Oak Forest, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Homewood Disposal Service (HDS): trash weekly, landscape waste April to November, recycling every two weeks.

Exact wording: “Homewood Disposal Service (HDS) provides refuse service for the City of Oak Forest. This program includes trash (weekly), landscape waste (April-November) and recycling (every two weeks).”

Source: www.oak-forest.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Homewood Disposal's Oak Forest page says disposal of one large item each week (for example a sofa, mattress or cabinet) is included with service. It does not mention calling ahead. The city's own page words this differently (unlimited garbage collection, furniture and appliances included).

Exact wording: “Tossing a sofa, mattress or cabinet? The disposal of one large item each week is included with your service.”

Source: mydisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

The city says furniture and appliances are included in the normal collection (stated in its flood disposal note). The page does not mention stickers, fees or freon removal.

Exact wording: “Furniture and appliances are included in the normal collection.”

Source: www.oak-forest.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

April 1 through November 30, on your regular garbage day. Use brown paper yard waste bags, bundles tied with twine or string, or a rented 95-gallon HDS yard waste cart. Branches cut to 3 to 4 foot lengths, no wider than 6 inches. "Yard waste only" stickers are needed only when your regular 35-gallon garbage cart is used just for yard waste; stickers are sold at the Clerk's Office in City Hall. No dirt or rock.

Source: www.oak-forest.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Scheduled curbside e-waste pickup through Homewood Disposal for residents under the city contract: call 708-798-1004 or submit an online request, then set items out on your regular pickup day. Unscheduled electronics are left behind. Limit of two TVs or computer monitors per address per year.

Source: www.oak-forest.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

The city points residents to the household hazardous waste drop-off at 156 Fort Hill Drive, Naperville, open Saturdays and Sundays 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. (excluding holidays). Latex paint is not hazardous; it must be hardened before it goes in the trash.

Exact wording: “The closest Household Hazardous Waste drop-off facility is located at 156 Fort Hill Drive in Naperville, IL 60540. The facility is open Saturdays and Sundays (excluding holidays), from 9 a.m. - 2 p.m. ... Please note that latex paint is not a hazardous material. To discard leftover latex paint in the trash, it must first be hardened.”

Source: www.oak-forest.org, checked Oct 9, 2026
