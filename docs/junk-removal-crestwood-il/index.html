What Crestwood's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Crestwood's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the village's own site or its hauler's page for Crestwood, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Republic Services, free to all Crestwood residents, with two 95-gallon carts per home (garbage and recycling).

Exact wording: “All Crestwood residents are provided garbage and recycling services from Republic Services for FREE! Each residence is provided with two large 95-gallon tote carts; one for garbage and one for recycling.”

Source: crestwood.illinois.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

One bulk item per week may go out with the garbage, which is collected Mondays.

Exact wording: “Garbage pickup in the Village of Crestwood is every Monday between 7:00 am & 7:00 pm. ... Residents are allowed to put one bulk item per week out with garbage.”

Source: crestwood.illinois.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Yard waste pickup started April 6, 2026 (no end date given). Use biodegradable paper lawn bags or a standard-size garbage can labeled 'Yard Waste Only'; yard waste labels are available at Village Hall, 13800 S. Cicero Ave. Trimmed branches must be bundled in 3 to 4 foot sections and piled neatly at the curb.

Source: crestwood.illinois.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

The village notes it is illegal in Illinois to put certain electronics in the trash and points residents to the Illinois EPA Beyond the Bin map of residential e-waste collection sites, with advice to call the collector first. E-waste was also taken at the village spring clean-up.

Exact wording: “Did you know that it is illegal in Illinois to throw certain electronic devices into the trash? ... Beyond the Bin Map of Residential E-Waste Collection Locations ... Note: It is important to call the collector to ensure they accept your device type as retailers do not have to accept all device types.”

Source: crestwood.illinois.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Crestwood

Crestwood Village Hall, (708) 371-4800, or Republic Services, (708) 385-8252

Source: crestwood.illinois.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

We could not confirm Crestwood's current rules on appliances, remodeling debris, hazardous waste. Ask Crestwood Village Hall, (708) 371-4800, or Republic Services, (708) 385-8252, before you set those out.
