Yard waste has its own rules

Since July 1, 1990, Illinois law has kept landscape waste (leaves, grass clippings and brush) out of landfill-bound garbage. Yard waste is collected separately, and each town sets its own season and bag or sticker rules. Your town's rules are on its page in our service area where we could confirm them. When the pile is bigger than a few bags, or it is mixed with fencing, a swing set or other junk, we haul the whole thing in one trip.

Sources:

  • Illinois law: 415 ILCS 5/22.22 (landscape waste)
