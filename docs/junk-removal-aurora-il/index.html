What Aurora's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Aurora's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the city's own site or its hauler's page for Aurora, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Groot is the City of Aurora's waste hauler for trash and recycling, with five collection options for residents.

Exact wording: “The City of Aurora's updated trash and recycling program offers 5 different collection options through its waste hauler, Groot.”

Source: www.aurora.il.us, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Single bulky items such as chairs, tables and mattresses need one garbage sticker on each item. Material that does not fit in a cart must be tied in bundles no more than 2 feet in diameter, 4 feet long and 50 pounds, with one sticker on each bundle. Questions go to Groot at 630-892-9294.

Source: www.aurora.il.us, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

Appliances such as refrigerators, stoves and water heaters must be scheduled in advance with Groot at 630-892-9294, and each appliance needs one garbage sticker.

Exact wording: “The collection of appliances such as refrigerators, stoves, water heaters, etc. must be scheduled in advance by calling Groot at 630-892-9294. Appliances must have 1 sticker affixed.”

Source: www.aurora.il.us, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Yard waste is collected from the first Monday in April to the first Friday in December. Use 30-gallon Kraft paper yard waste bags of 50 pounds or less, each with a waste sticker (the same stickers used for extra garbage).

Exact wording: “Yard waste collection begins the first Monday in April and ends the first Friday in December. Yard waste must be placed in a 30-gallon Kraft paper yard waste bag not to exceed 50 pounds and have a waste sticker attached. The stickers used for yard waste are the same as the stickers used for excess garbage.”

Source: www.aurora.il.us, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Electronics are banned from Illinois landfills, so they cannot go out with the trash. The City of Aurora holds electronics collection events; call the City's Customer Service Center at 630-256-4636 for details.

Exact wording: “Effective on January 1, 2012, electronic waste is banned from Illinois landfills. The City of Aurora offers collection events. For more information on electronics recycling, call the Customer Service Center number at 630-256-4636.”

Source: www.aurora.il.us, checked Oct 9, 2026

Remodeling and construction debris

Small amounts of construction debris and rolls of carpet are picked up if they are tied in bundles no more than 2 feet in diameter, 4 feet long and 50 pounds, with a garbage sticker on each bundle.

Exact wording: “Materials that do not fit in a cart or container, such as small amounts of construction debris and rolls of carpeting, must be securely tied in bundles not exceeding 2 feet in diameter, 4 feet in length, and 50 pounds in weight. A sticker must be affixed to each bundle.”

Source: www.aurora.il.us, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

The City directs residents to the household hazardous waste drop-off facility at 156 Fort Hill Drive in Naperville, open Saturdays and Sundays from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., except holidays.

Exact wording: “The facility is located at: 156 Fort Hill Drive Naperville, IL 60540 It will operate on Saturdays and Sundays, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., excluding holidays.”

Source: www.aurora.il.us, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Aurora

Groot, 630-892-9294 (the number the City gives for bulk items)

Source: www.aurora.il.us, checked Oct 9, 2026
