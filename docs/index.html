FAQ

Questions people ask

How much does junk removal cost?

It depends on how much there is, what it is, and how hard it is to get out. Call or text a photo to (708) 247-6440 and we will give you a straight price before we start. See how pricing works.

Do I have to bring everything outside?

No. We carry it out from wherever it is: basement, attic, upstairs, garage or backyard.

What do you not take?

Household hazardous waste like wet paint, solvents, chemicals, motor oil and propane tanks. See what we take for the full list and where those items can go.

Do you do junk pickup for just one item?

Yes. A single couch, mattress or fridge is a normal call.

Do you offer junk removal near me?

If you are in Chicago Ridge, the southwest suburbs or on Chicago's Southwest Side, yes. Find your town on the service area page.
