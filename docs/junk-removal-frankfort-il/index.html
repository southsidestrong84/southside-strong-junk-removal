What Frankfort's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Frankfort's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the village's own site or its hauler's page for Frankfort, linked under each rule.

Frankfort's village pages and Homewood Disposal's pages differ on some details, including the yard waste dates. Check with Homewood before you set items out. Frankfort Square, Frankfort Township and unincorporated homes have different service.

Who collects the garbage

Homewood Disposal, which collects garbage at the curb on Mondays.

Exact wording: “The garbage collector, Homewood Disposal, picks up at the curb on Monday.”

Source: www.frankfortil.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Each household is allowed one large item per week, along with three garbage cans.

Exact wording: “Each household is allowed three garbage cans and one large item per week.”

Source: www.frankfortil.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Yard waste season typically runs from the first Monday in April to the last Monday in November, with Monday pickup by Homewood Disposal. Yard waste goes in separate recyclable bags, each with a yard waste sticker. Stickers are sold at the Village Administration Building.

Exact wording: “All yard Waste materials must be placed in separate recyclable bags labeled with the waste program sticker, ready for garbage collection by Homewood Disposal on Mondays. Yard waste stickers are available for purchase at the Village Administration Building. Yard waste season typically begins the first Monday of April and typically ends the last Monday in November.”

Source: www.frankfortil.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Will County's year-round electronics drop-off at Frankfort Township, 11000 W. Lincoln Highway, is open the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of each month, 5 p.m. to 7 p.m., closed on holidays (815-469-4907). It accepts all electronics, damaged or obsolete, from residents of any community fully or partly in Will County. Bring ID; the limit is 3 TVs and 3 monitors per vehicle.

Source: www.willcountygreen.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

Will County helps fund the permanent Naperville Regional household hazardous waste drop-off at 156 Fort Hill Drive, Naperville, open Saturday and Sunday, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., every weekend except holidays.

Exact wording: “Will County also helps to fund a permanent HHW Drop-Off open every weekend (except holidays): Naperville Regional HHW 156 Fort Hill Drive (as of February 21, 2015) Naperville, IL (prefer no phone calls) Saturday & Sunday 9am-2pm”

Source: www.willcountygreen.com, checked Oct 9, 2026
