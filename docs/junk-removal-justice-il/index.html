What Justice's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Justice's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the village's own site or its hauler's page for Justice, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Groot Industries, the village waste collector, 888-485-0900.

Exact wording: “Groot Industries (Village Waste Collector) 888.485.0900”

Source: www.villageofjustice.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Up to two bulk items free on each regular garbage day (couches, dressers, cabinets, dining tables and chairs). Three or more in a week are charged per item, paid before pickup. Carpeting costs extra and must be cut into 4-foot lengths and rolled. Call Groot toll free at 888-485-0900 to arrange pickup.

Source: www.villageofjustice.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

Groot's Justice page counts white goods such as stoves and refrigerators among the two free bulk items per pickup. Schedule appliance pickup in advance; the page lists 708-485-0900 for e-waste and appliances (and 888-485-0900 toll free for bulk pickups). No freon or sticker rule is stated.

Exact wording: “Free bulk item pickup means the free collection on each occasion of up to two (2) bulk items, including without limitation, couches, dressers, cabinets, dining room table and chairs, and/or household appliances commonly known as 'white goods', including without limitation, stoves and refrigerators. ... For E-waste and appliance pick up, please call us at 708-485-0900 to schedule in advance.”

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Groot collects unlimited yard waste April 1 through November 30, on Mondays (per the village brochure). Grass, leaves and garden debris go in kraft paper bags, rolled closed and under 50 pounds. Brush and branches up to 6 inches thick must be tied with twine (no wire or plastic), 4 feet or shorter and 2 feet or less across.

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Groot offers at-home e-waste pickup for an additional fee; call ahead to schedule (708-485-0900). The page notes Illinois law bars mixing electronics into landfill trash.

Source: www.groot.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Justice

Groot, 888-485-0900, or Justice Village Hall, 708-458-2520

Source: www.villageofjustice.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

We could not confirm Justice's current rules on remodeling debris, hazardous waste. Ask Groot, 888-485-0900, or Justice Village Hall, 708-458-2520, before you set those out.
