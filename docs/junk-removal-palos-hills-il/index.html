What Palos Hills's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Palos Hills's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the city's own site or its hauler's page for Palos Hills, linked under each rule.

Several of these rules come from City documents that are a few years old (2018 and 2021). Check with the City before you set items out.

Who collects the garbage

Republic Services (Allied Waste). The City announced a five-year extension of single-family scavenger service on December 29, 2021.

Exact wording: “The City of Palos Hills and Allied Waste / Republic Services recently agreed to extend scavenger services for single family homes for five more years.”

Source: www.paloshillsweb.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Republic picks up bulk items (furniture, large household goods, appliances) at no cost; the City letter states no item limit or call-ahead rule.

Exact wording: “Republic Services will continue to service bulk items at no cost including furniture and large household goods, but not limited to washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, microwaves, and other appliances.”

Source: www.paloshillsweb.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

Washers, dryers, dishwashers, microwaves and other appliances are collected at no cost as bulk items; chlorofluorocarbons (Freon) must be removed before pickup.

Exact wording: “Republic Services will continue to service bulk items at no cost including furniture and large household goods, but not limited to washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, microwaves, and other appliances. All bulk items containing chlorofluorocarbons must be removed prior to being service.”

Source: www.paloshillsweb.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

April 1 through November 30. Use 30-gallon Kraft paper bags, or an unlined 33-gallon homeowner container with a vendor-supplied yard waste sticker on the outside; 35 lbs maximum per can.

Exact wording: “Republic Services will continue to provide the residents yard waste service from April 1, through November 30th of each year under the current format of 30 gallon craft paper bags, or unlined 33-gallon container provided by the homeowner with a vendor supplied yard waste sticker affixed to the outside of the container. The max allowable weight is 35 pounds per can.”

Source: www.paloshillsweb.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Free curbside e-waste pickup on normal pickup days; schedule ahead with Republic at (708) 385-8252 or Paloshills@republicservices.com (no same-day pickup).

Exact wording: “Republic Services will continue to do free curbside collection of E Waste on normal pickup days. Pickup needs to be scheduled ahead of time with a call at (708) 385-8252 or an email at (Paloshills@republicservices.com). Resident name, address, and the item to be picked up need to be indicated on any messages; same day pickup is not allowed.”

Source: www.paloshillsweb.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

Paint, gasoline and similar items cannot go in household trash. The City points residents to two permanent drop-off sites: Naperville (156 Fort Hill Drive, 630-420-6095) and Chicago (1150 N. North Branch on Goose Island, 312-744-7672), and says to call to verify hours.

Source: www.paloshillsweb.org, checked Oct 9, 2026
