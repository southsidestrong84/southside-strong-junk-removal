What Tinley Park's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Tinley Park's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the village's own site or its hauler's page for Tinley Park, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Homewood Disposal Service, under a village contract effective Jan. 20, 2026. As of Oct. 1, 2026 the rate is $31.16 per month for residents ($29.66 for seniors 65 and older, $0 for certified 100% disabled veterans).

Source: www.tinleypark.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Homewood Disposal's Tinley Park page says disposal of one large item each week (such as a sofa, mattress or cabinet) is included with service. It does not mention calling ahead. Oversized items (pianos, hot tubs, spas) cost $45 each under the village contract.

Exact wording: “Tossing a sofa, mattress or cabinet? The disposal of one large item each week is included with your service.”

Source: mydisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

April 1 to November 30. No yard waste stickers needed in Tinley Park. Use brown paper yard waste bags or rent a 95-gallon yard waste cart. Branches cut to 3 to 4 foot lengths, no wider than 3 inches, bagged or bundled with twine or string. Yard waste may not be mixed with garbage.

Source: mydisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Schedule e-waste pickup with Homewood Disposal (HDS) before setting items out. Limit of two TVs or monitors per single-family home per year.

Exact wording: “Residents can receive e-waste collection as needed, which must be scheduled with HDS prior to collection. Residents are limited to two TVs or monitors per single-family home per year.”

Source: www.tinleypark.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

The village names two household hazardous waste sites (in its gasoline disposal note): Naperville Regional HHW, 156 Fort Hill Drive, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. Saturdays and Sundays; and Chicago Regional HHW, 1150 N North Branch on Goose Island, (312) 744-7672, 7 a.m. to noon Tuesdays, 2 to 7 p.m. Thursdays, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. on the first Saturday of the month.

Exact wording: “Take gas to one of the two Household Hazardous Waste Collection sites in the area: Naperville Regional HHW 156 Fort Hill Drive, Naperville, IL 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. Saturdays and Sundays Chicago Regional HHW 1150 N North Branch on Goose Island, Chicago, IL (312) 744-7672 7 a.m. to noon Tuesdays 2 to 7 p.m. Thursdays 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. the first Saturday of the month”

Source: www.tinleypark.org, checked Oct 9, 2026
