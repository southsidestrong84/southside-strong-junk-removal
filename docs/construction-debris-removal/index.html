Construction debris removal questions

Can my garbage hauler take remodeling debris?

Usually only in small amounts, if at all, and the rules change from town to town. Check your town on our service area pages.

Do you take paint and solvents?

No. Wet paint, solvents and chemicals are household hazardous waste. See what we take for where they can go.
