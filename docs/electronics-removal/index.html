Why electronics cannot go in the trash

In Illinois it is illegal to throw TVs, computers, monitors, printers and a list of other electronics in the trash. State law bans them from landfills, and Illinois EPA's current list, effective January 1, 2026, also covers home audio such as speakers, stereos and amps. The ban applies to businesses as well as households. These devices have to be recycled instead.

If you would rather drop electronics off yourself, Cook County's CHaRM recycling center at 15800 State St. in South Holland takes TVs, monitors and other electronics from residential customers, and many towns run their own collection, listed on our town pages where we could confirm it.

Sources:

  • Illinois EPA: electronics recycling
  • Illinois EPA: electronics recycling FAQ
  • Illinois law: 415 ILCS 151/1-83 (electronics landfill ban)
  • Cook County: CHaRM recycling center

TV and electronics removal questions

Will you take an old tube TV?

Yes. Old tube TVs are heavy and awkward, and they are exactly what people get stuck with. We carry them out.

Can I put a TV out with the garbage?

Not in Illinois. Covered electronics like TVs and monitors are banned from landfills. See the rule above, or check your town's electronics drop-off on our service area pages.
