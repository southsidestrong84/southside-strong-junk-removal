Why a fridge is not just scrap metal

Household refrigerators, freezers and window air conditioners usually still hold their refrigerant when they are thrown out. Federal rules make the last stop in the disposal chain, such as a scrap metal recycler or a landfill, responsible for making sure that refrigerant is recovered first. Illinois law adds that nobody may collect appliances for landfill disposal until the "white good components" are removed: refrigerant, mercury switches and parts that may contain PCBs.

That is why an old fridge cannot ride along with the garbage. We take appliances where they can be handled properly.

Sources:

  • US EPA: Section 608 safe disposal requirements
  • Illinois law: 415 ILCS 5/22.28 (white goods)

Disconnecting first

We haul appliances that are already disconnected. If a gas range, a gas dryer or a hard-wired water heater is still hooked up, have a plumber or electrician shut it off and cap it before we arrive. Water lines on washers and fridges should be shut off and drained so nothing leaks on the way out.

From the basement or the second floor

Basement chest freezers and second-floor laundry machines are where people get hurt. We bring the equipment to move them safely and protect floors and stairs on the way out.

Appliance removal questions

Do I need to defrost the freezer first?

Please do, the day before if you can. Empty it, unplug it and leave the door open so it drains. It keeps water off your floors on the way out.

Will my village pick up an old refrigerator?

Some towns do, often with a sticker, a fee or a phone call first, and some do not take items that hold refrigerant at all. Look up your town on our service area pages for the exact rule, or call us and we will take it from wherever it sits.
