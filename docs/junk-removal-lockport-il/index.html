What Lockport's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Lockport's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the city's own site or its hauler's page for Lockport, linked under each rule.

The City's pages and WM's give different yard waste dates and differ on whether appliances need a call first. Call WM before you set them out.

Who collects the garbage

Waste Management (WM), under an inclusive contract with the City.

Exact wording: “The city has an inclusive contract with Waste Management.”

Source: www.cityoflockport.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Furniture is collected on your regular pickup day under the City's inclusive WM contract, but only one large item is collected per week.

Exact wording: “The city has an inclusive contract with Waste Management. Furniture and appliances will be collected on your regular pick-up day however, only one large item will be collected per week.”

Source: www.cityoflockport.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

Appliances are collected on your regular pickup day, but only one large item (an appliance or a piece of furniture) is collected per week.

Exact wording: “The city has an inclusive contract with Waste Management. Furniture and appliances will be collected on your regular pick-up day however, only one large item will be collected per week.”

Source: www.cityoflockport.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Will County's year-round electronics drop-off at the City of Lockport site, 17112 Prime Blvd (east of I-355), is open Tuesdays and Fridays only, 6 a.m. to 11 a.m., closed on holidays (815-838-0549). It accepts all electronics, damaged or obsolete, from residents of any community fully or partly in Will County. Bring ID; the limit is 3 TVs and 3 monitors per vehicle.

Source: www.willcountygreen.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Remodeling and construction debris

WM collects construction and demolition debris from do-it-yourself remodeling projects, up to 1 cubic yard. Bundled wood and other debris must be 4 feet or shorter, and smaller debris must be in a rigid can or container.

Exact wording: “Waste Management will collect construction / demolition debris from do-it-yourself remodeling projects; however, it is limited to 1 cubic yard. Bundled wood and other debris cannot exceed 4 feet in length, and smaller debris must be in a rigid can or container.”

Source: www.cityoflockport.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

Paint products, insecticides, oil, antifreeze, car batteries and other hazardous waste are not collected at the curb. To find a drop-off location, the City refers residents to the Will County Land Use Department (815-774-3321) or the Illinois EPA (217-782-9285).

Source: www.cityoflockport.net, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Lockport

the City of Lockport Water Department, 815-838-0549 (option 3), or WM after hours, 800-796-9696

Source: www.cityoflockport.net, checked Oct 9, 2026
