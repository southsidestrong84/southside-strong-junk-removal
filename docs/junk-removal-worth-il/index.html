What Worth's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Worth's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the village's own site or its hauler's page for Worth, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Lakeshore Recycling Systems (LRS), under contract with the Village.

Exact wording: “The Village of Worth currently contracts with Lakeshore Recycling Systems (LRS) garbage company.”

Source: www.villageofworth.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Bulk items that do not fit in the cart (couches, tables, chairs, desks, large toys) are picked up on the regular service day, with no item limit stated. Items over 50 lbs or longer than 6 feet need a scheduled special pickup.

Exact wording: “Bulk items that don’t fit into your garbage cart are collected your regular day of service. Examples include couches, tables, chairs, desks, large toys, etc. Schedule special pick up for bulk items exceeding 50lbs, or longer than 6’ L”

Source: www.lrsrecycles.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

LRS takes white goods such as refrigerators and air conditioners only if they are free of CFC/HCFC refrigerants, PCBs, mercury switches and other hazardous components, and only when prescheduled and prepaid with LRS customer service (no price published).

Exact wording: ““White Goods” are appliances that contain CFC, HCFC, PCBs, mercury switches or other hazardous components (refrigerators, air conditioners, humidifiers etc). These items will be taken but will need to be free of the above referenced material and required to be prescheduled and prepaid to LRS customer service.”

Source: www.lrsrecycles.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Yard waste service runs April 1 through November 30. Use brown Kraft paper bags, or your own cans (35 gallons and 40 lbs maximum) marked with a large "X".

Exact wording: “Yard waste service is provided from April 1 through November 30. Yard waste can be placed at your pickup point in either brown Kraft Paper Bags, or in your own containers that cannot exceed 35 gallons in size, or 40 pounds in weight. Put a large "X" on your yard waste cans.”

Source: www.villageofworth.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

LRS offers paid curbside e-waste pickup for Worth households with LRS service; it must be prescheduled and prepaid.

Exact wording: “LRS provides a curb side home collection of E-Waste for Village of Worth residents that have LRS waste and recycling collection. This service provides a more convenient and simple way to responsibly manage electronics that are banned from the landfill. Scheduling items to be removed would need a prescheduling and prepayment made to have the items removed.”

Source: www.lrsrecycles.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Remodeling and construction debris

Home remodeling waste (drywall, plywood, paneling, lumber, doors, windows, cabinets, porcelain fixtures, sod, fencing, concrete, rocks) is not regular pickup: it needs a scheduled special pickup, with an estimate and a charge by the yard.

Exact wording: “Home remodeling waste includes, but is not limited to drywall, plywood, paneling, lumber, doors, windows, cabinets, porcelain fixtures, sod, fencing, concrete, or rocks will require special pickups. Call to schedule service and receive instructions on how to prepare them for collection. Estimates will be provided, and items will be charged by the yard.”

Source: www.lrsrecycles.com, checked Oct 9, 2026
