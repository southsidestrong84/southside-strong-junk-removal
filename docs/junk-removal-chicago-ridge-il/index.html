What Chicago Ridge's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Chicago Ridge's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the village's own site or its hauler's page for Chicago Ridge, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Flood Brothers Disposal/Recycling, under contract with the Village for single-family and two-flat refuse pickup (service day: Monday).

Exact wording: “The Village has a contract with Flood Brothers Disposal/Recycling for residential and two-flat apartment refuse pick-up. All items for collection must be placed at the curb by 6:00 a.m. Service Days are Mondays.”

Source: www.chicagoridge.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

One bulk item per week (mattresses, box springs, tables, chairs, other furniture) is picked up free with regular refuse service. Items must be 50 lbs or less; heavier items need a special pickup scheduled with Flood Brothers.

Exact wording: “As part of the regularly scheduled refuse collection, Flood Brothers will collect one (1) bulk item each week at no additional charge. Examples include mattresses, box springs, tables chairs and other furniture. Items must not exceed 50 lbs. Bulk items weighting more than 50 lbs require a special pickup. Please call Flood Brothers to schedule items of this size.”

Source: www.chicagoridge.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

White goods (refrigerators, stoves, washers, dishwashers, freezers, dryers, water heaters) are picked up on the regular collection day for $12.00 per item; call Flood Brothers 24 hours ahead and remove refrigerator and freezer doors. The page does not address Freon removal.

Source: www.floodbrothersdisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Collected on the regular pickup day from the first full service week in April through the last week of November; each household gets one 95-gallon yard waste cart.

Exact wording: “Yard waste collection takes place on the same day as trash and recycling pickups, beginning the first full service week in April and ending the last week of November, annually. Each household will be provided a single, 95-gallon container for yard waste.”

Source: www.chicagoridge.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Flood Brothers collects electronic recyclables twice a year; the Village and Flood Brothers announce the details each year.

Exact wording: “Collection service is provided for electronic recyclables twice per year. Details will be provided annually by both The Village of Chicago Ridge as well as Flood Brothers.”

Source: www.floodbrothersdisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

For old gasoline, oil or paint, the Village FAQ points residents to the Household Hazardous Waste Facility in Naperville (156 Fort Hill Dr., 630-420-6095).

Exact wording: “There is also a Household Hazardous Waste Facility in Naperville you can contact. They are located at 156 Fort Hill Dr. Naperville, IL 60540. You can reach them at 630-420-6095 or on their website.”

Source: chicagoridge.org, checked Oct 9, 2026
