What Palos Heights's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Palos Heights's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm on the village's own pages.

Who collects the garbage

Republic Services, under a City contract covering single-family homes (not condominiums or multi-unit buildings). Pickup is Thursday citywide.

Exact wording: “Residential Garbage Pickup for the Entire City is on Thursday. The City of Palos Heights has a contract with Republic Services for the collection and disposal of solid waste from all single-family residences within the corporate limits, except for condominiums and multi-unit buildings.”

Source: palosheights.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

The City's Republic contract (as amended in 2022) lists bulky items among services outside the contract's scope, which Republic may provide under separate agreements with residents. The City's garbage page does not describe a bulk pickup. (White goods are covered separately, see appliances.)

Source: palosheights.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

Republic collects one white good per home per week under the City contract. White goods are large appliances such as refrigerators, dishwashers, water heaters, washers and dryers. The contract's bulky waste definition lists refrigerators with all CFC (freon) removed. The contract text does not mention a sticker or fee.

Exact wording: “3.15 White Goods. The Contractor shall also provide collection services for one (1) white good item per week per Residential Unit. ... 1.30 White Goods. Large appliances such as refrigerators, dish washers, water heaters, washers, dryers. ... Stoves, refrigerators (with all CFC removed), water tanks, washing machines, furniture and other similar items”

Source: palosheights.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

April 1 through November 30. Kraft paper bags may be used instead of or along with cans, up to 45 gallons or 35 pounds per bag. Yard waste in plastic bags is not collected. (Free Republic yard waste stickers and an optional $3.00 a month yard waste cart come from the 2022 contract amendment.)

Exact wording: “Yard Waste pick-up runs from April 1st through November 30th. Kraft paper bags of degradable material may be used in place of or in addition to the can system. Such bags, when filled, must not exceed forty-five gallons in capacity or 35 pounds, which is greater, and must be capable of being carried and loaded without breakage. Yard waste in plastic bags will not be collected.”

Source: palosheights.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

The City holds two e-waste collection days a year, in spring and fall.

Exact wording: “The City of Palos Heights has 2 E-Waste Collection Days per year Spring/Fall.”

Source: palosheights.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Remodeling and construction debris

Small amounts of remodeling debris go out free with regular pickup: containers of 35 gallons or less under 50 pounds, or 4-foot bundles under 50 pounds secured on both sides, up to the equivalent of 5 normal refuse containers. Anything more needs a special pickup arranged by the resident (Republic gives a cost estimate on request; residents may use another hauler).

Source: palosheights.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

The City points residents to state household hazardous waste options: Illinois-sponsored one-day drop-off events each spring and fall, and permanent facilities in Chicago (Goose Island, 1150 N. Branch Street, 312-744-7672), Naperville (156 Fort Hill Drive, 630-420-6095) and Rockford (815-967-6737). Call before you go.

Source: palosheights.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Palos Heights

Palos Heights Public Works, 708-361-1806, or Republic Services, 708-385-8252

Source: palosheights.org, checked Oct 9, 2026
