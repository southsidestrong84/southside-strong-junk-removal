What Evergreen Park's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Evergreen Park's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm on the village's own pages.

Who collects the garbage

Flood Brothers Disposal, under contract with the village for trash, recycling and yard waste.

Exact wording: “The village's trash, recycling, and yard waste removal is contracted to Flood Brothers Disposal.”

Source: www.evergreenpark-ill.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Bulk items under 50 pounds (mattresses, box springs, small tables, chairs, other small furniture) are collected weekly on your regular day at no extra charge. Items over 50 pounds need a call to Flood Brothers at 630-261-0400 at least 48 hours ahead to schedule a special pickup.

Exact wording: “Bulk items weighing less than 50 pounds will be collected each week on your collection day at no additional charge. Examples of bulk items include mattresses, box springs, small tables, chairs, and other small furniture. For bulk items weighing more than 50 pounds, please call Flood Brothers at 630-261-0400 at least 48 hours prior to schedule a special pick-up.”

Source: www.evergreenpark-ill.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

One white good per week (refrigerators, dishwashers, water heaters, washers, dryers) is collected free. Call Flood Brothers at 630-261-0400 48 hours before collection day to schedule. The village page does not mention a sticker or freon removal.

Exact wording: “Collection for one white good item per week will be collected for no charge. Example white goods items include: refrigerators, dishwashers, water heaters, washers, dryers, etc. Residents must contact Flood Brothers at 630-261-0400 48 hours before collection day to schedule a pick-up.”

Source: www.evergreenpark-ill.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Weekly from April 1 through December 15, on the same day as trash and recycling, in the green yard waste can. Extra yard waste goes in a covered plastic or metal container or a biodegradable kraft bag, no larger than 35 gallons and no heavier than 50 pounds. The village page does not mention stickers.

Exact wording: “Yard waste will be picked up weekly from April 1 through December 15 on the same day as your refuse and recycling collection. Yard waste should be placed in the green can. Additional yard waste shall be placed in a plastic or metal container with a tight cover, or a biodegradable kraft bag, of a size no larger than 35 gallons and not exceeding 50 pounds.”

Source: www.evergreenpark-ill.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Flood Brothers picks up electronics weekly by appointment. Call 630-261-0400 48 hours before your collection day.

Exact wording: “Weekly collection will be provided for electronic recyclables. Residents must contact Flood Brothers at 630-261-0400 48 hours before collection day to schedule a pick-up.”

Source: www.evergreenpark-ill.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Remodeling and construction debris

Small do-it-yourself project debris is collected free, up to five normal refuse containers. It must be in containers of 35 gallons or less and 50 pounds or less, or tied in bundles no longer than 4 feet and no heavier than 50 pounds. Anything more needs a scheduled special collection through Flood Brothers.

Source: www.floodbrothersdisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

Call Flood Brothers at 630-261-0400 at least 48 hours ahead about hazardous items such as paint, pesticides, poison, cleaning solvents, oil, gasoline and other automotive fluids. Flood Brothers works with residents on disposal using Cook County recycling programs.

Source: www.evergreenpark-ill.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Evergreen Park

Flood Brothers, 630-261-0400, or Evergreen Park Public Works, (708) 229-3350

Source: www.evergreenpark-ill.com, checked Oct 9, 2026
