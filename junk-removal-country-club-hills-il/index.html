What Country Club Hills's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Country Club Hills's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the city's own site or its hauler's page for Country Club Hills, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Homewood Disposal, under city contract, for all single-family homes. Multi-family developments contract privately (residents there contact their homeowners association).

Exact wording: “Garbage removal and curbside recycling service for all single-family homes is contracted by Homewood Disposal throughout the City. Multi-family housing developments are privately contracted so in that case contact your homeowners association.”

Source: countryclubhills.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Homewood Disposal's Country Club Hills page says disposal of one large item each week (such as a sofa, mattress or cabinet) is included with service. It does not mention calling ahead.

Exact wording: “Tossing a sofa, mattress or cabinet? The disposal of one large item each week is included with your service.”

Source: mydisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

April 1 to November 30. All yard waste goes in brown paper yard waste bags, each with a yard waste sticker (bags and stickers sold at most local grocery and hardware stores). 95-gallon yard waste carts can be rented. Branches cut to 3 to 4 foot lengths, no wider than 6 inches; any bundle outside a bag needs its own sticker. Homewood says stickers will no longer be required starting April 2027.

Source: countryclubhills.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Homewood Disposal's Country Club Hills page tells residents to schedule a special electronics pickup with Homewood. It does not state a fee or item limit.

Exact wording: “Many electronics have been banned from landfills. Schedule a special pick up with us.”

Source: mydisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026
