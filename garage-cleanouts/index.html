Garage clean up services, start to finish

  1. Walk through. You show us what stays and what goes. Mark the keep pile or just tell us as we work.
  2. We load. We carry out everything that goes, including the heavy stuff: old freezers, mowers, workbenches, shelving.
  3. We sweep. When the junk is gone, the floor gets swept and you get your parking spot back.

What we take from garages

  • Old mowers, snowblowers and tools
  • Shelving, cabinets and workbenches
  • Bikes, sports gear and toys
  • Old freezers and fridges
  • Boxes, bins and old storage
  • Tires (ask us first)
  • Scrap metal

What has to go another way

Garages collect the things we cannot put in a truck: half-full cans of paint, motor oil, pesticides, pool chemicals and propane tanks. Set them aside and see what we take for where they can go. Everything else, we handle.
