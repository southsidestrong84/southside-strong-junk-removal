What Calumet City's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Calumet City's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm on the village's own pages.

Who collects the garbage

Republic Services, (708) 385-8252. The city is negotiating with its refuse provider and has put residential solid waste collection out for proposals in 2026, so the hauler or terms may change.

Exact wording: “Republic Services will only pick up 1 bulk item PER WEEK. Other refuse must be placed in receptacle. Republic Services (708) 385-8252”

Source: calumetcity.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

One large item per week goes through Republic: call Republic the day before your regular garbage day. For three or more bulk items, contact Calumet City Public Works.

Exact wording: “For bulk item pickup (three or more), please contact Public Works. For single large item pickup (one item per week), kindly contact Republic Services the day prior to your regular garbage pickup.”

Source: calumetcity.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Branch (limb) pickup: call Public Works and say whether the branches are on the parkway or in the alley. Public Works also lists leaf collection among its services. No season dates or bag, sticker or cart rules were found on the city pages checked.

Exact wording: “For limb pickup, please contact Public Works and indicate whether the branches are located on the parkway or in the alley.”

Source: calumetcity.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Calumet City

Calumet City Public Works, (708) 891-8161

Source: calumetcity.org, checked Oct 9, 2026

We could not confirm Calumet City's current rules on appliances, electronics, remodeling debris, hazardous waste. Ask Calumet City Public Works, (708) 891-8161, before you set those out.
