Above-ground pool removal

The pool has had its last summer. We take it down, haul away the walls, liner, frame, ladder and pump, and leave the area clean. One customer summed it up: the area "was cleaned up with no debris left."

What comes out

  • Steel or resin walls and top rails
  • Uprights and bottom track
  • Liner
  • Ladders and steps
  • Pump, filter and hoses
  • Pool decks and stairs around the pool (see light demolition)

Before we come

Drain the pool. Many towns have rules on where pool water can go, so check with your village before you pump it into the street or a neighbor's yard. Shut off and unplug the pump, and have an electrician disconnect it if it is hard-wired.

Above-ground pool removal questions

Do I need to drain it first?

Yes, please drain it before we arrive, and follow your village's rules on where the water can go.

Do you take the deck around the pool too?

We can. Tell us about it when you ask for the estimate so we plan the time and the truck space.
