What Burbank's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Burbank's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm from official sources: the city's own site or its hauler's page for Burbank, linked under each rule.

Who collects the garbage

Burbank's City website sends residents to Lakeshore Recycling Systems (LRS) to pay the garbage bill and lists LRS in its resource directory.

Exact wording: “LRS | Pay Garbage Bill”

Source: www.burbankil.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Picked up weekly on the regular garbage day from April 1 through the last pickup in November. Use paper yard waste bags or round Rubbermaid-type cans; overloaded cans are skipped, and branches under 3 feet must be bundled.

Exact wording: “Yard waste is picked up every week on your regularly scheduled garbage day from April 1 through your last pickup in November. Yard waste must be in paper yard waste bags or the round Rubbermaid-type containers. If the can is overloaded/too heavy, it will not be picked up. Small branches under 3 feet must be bundled, or they will not take them.”

Source: www.burbankil.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026
