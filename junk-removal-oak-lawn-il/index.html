What Oak Lawn's garbage pickup will take

Before you pay anyone to haul something away, it is worth knowing what Oak Lawn's regular garbage service will take. Here is what we could confirm on the village's own pages.

Who collects the garbage

Flood Brothers Disposal has been the Village waste hauler since October 1, 2025.

Exact wording: “As of October 1, 2025, Flood Brothers Disposal has officially taken over as the Village of Oak Lawn's waste hauler.”

Source: oaklawn-il.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Furniture and bulk items

Bulk items under 50 lbs (mattresses, box springs, small tables, chairs, small furniture) go out weekly on refuse day at no extra charge, with no item limit stated. Items over 50 lbs need a special pickup called in at least 24 hours ahead.

Source: www.floodbrothersdisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Appliances and anything with refrigerant

Appliances (refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, dishwashers, water heaters, washers, dryers) are picked up for a $20.00 fee billed by Flood Brothers. Call 24 hours ahead, remove refrigerator and freezer doors, and empty the unit; Flood Brothers removes any Freon after pickup and recycles the appliance.

Source: www.floodbrothersdisposal.com, checked Oct 9, 2026

Yard waste

Weekly yard waste pickup runs from the first full week of April through the second week of December.

Exact wording: “Yard waste pick-up runs from the first full week of April through the second week of December.”

Source: oaklawn-il.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

TVs and electronics

Flood Brothers picks up electronics for an extra $30 per pickup (up to 10 items, only one TV); schedule at least 24 hours ahead at (630) 261-0400.

Exact wording: “Flood Brothers will provide collection of electronic devices for an extra fee of $30, to include up to 10 items per pick-up, but only one (1) television set per pick-up. Residents need to contact Flood Brothers at (630) 261-0400 at least 24 hours in advance to schedule a pick-up.”

Source: oaklawn-il.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Remodeling and construction debris

Neither the Village nor Flood Brothers collects debris generated by contractors; the contractor must remove and dispose of it.

Exact wording: “Please note that neither the Village nor its waste hauler, Flood Brothers Disposal, is responsible for collecting debris generated by contractors. It is the contractor's responsibility to remove and dispose of any materials resulting from their work.”

Source: oaklawn-il.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Paint, chemicals and other hazardous waste

Flood Brothers picks up household hazardous waste (paint, pesticides, poison, cleaning solvents, oil, gasoline, automotive fluids) for $7 per item; schedule at least 24 hours ahead at (630) 261-0400.

Exact wording: “Flood Brothers will collect household hazardous waste for an extra fee of $7 per item, including paint, pesticides, poison, cleaning solvents, oil, gasoline, and other automotive fluids. Residents need to contact Flood Brothers at (630) 261-0400 at least 24 hours in advance to schedule a pickup.”

Source: oaklawn-il.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026

Who to call in Oak Lawn

Flood Brothers, (630) 261-0400, or Oak Lawn Public Works, (708) 499-7756

Source: oaklawn-il.gov, checked Oct 9, 2026
